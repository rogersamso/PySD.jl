Vensim functions

Each Vensim function: what it is for, its definition, and how to write it in PySD.jl (what the translator writes). They fall into groups by how they behave in time, which decides how they run with other solvers than Vensim's Euler steps (Solvers and the model's ticks):

  • Memory as a stock: hidden stocks with rates. Any solver integrates them.
  • Memory by counting steps: values at earlier steps of Δt. They move at the model's ticks, whatever the solver.
  • Switches at given times: functions of the time. The solver stops at the ticks, so a switch at a tick is exact.
  • Switches on values, and plain calculations: no time behaviour of their own.

In the definitions, $t_k = t_0 + k\,Δt$ are the ticks.

Memory as a stock

SMOOTH, SMOOTHI

SMOOTH(input, T) · SMOOTHI(input, T, initial_value)

Perception and adjustment: what people believe about a quantity lags behind its real value (perceived demand, an expected price, the average of a noisy series). Each unit of time the output closes a share 1/T of its gap to the input: after a jump, 63 % of the way in T, 95 % in 3T.

\[S(t_0) = \text{input}(t_0) \;\; (\text{SMOOTHI: initial\_value}), \qquad \frac{dS}{dt} = \frac{\text{input} - S}{T}, \qquad \text{SMOOTH} = S\]

PySD.jl: smooth(input, T), smooth(input, T; init = initial_value).

SMOOTH3, SMOOTH3I, SMOOTH N

SMOOTH3(input, T) · SMOOTH3I(input, T, initial_value) · SMOOTH N(input, T, initial_value, n)

The same through a chain of n smooths, each of time T/n: the output hardly reacts at first, then catches up (an S-shape). Information that goes through several stages: measured, reported, then acted on.

\[\frac{dS_1}{dt} = (\text{input} - S_1)\,\frac{n}{T}, \qquad \frac{dS_i}{dt} = (S_{i-1} - S_i)\,\frac{n}{T}, \qquad \text{output} = S_n\]

with every $S_i(t_0)$ the input's (or initial_value).

PySD.jl: smooth(input, T; order = 3), smooth(input, T; order = n, init = initial_value).

DELAY1, DELAY1I

DELAY1(input, T) · DELAY1I(input, T, initial_value)

Material in transit: goods being shipped, buildings under construction, people in training. Whatever goes in comes out later and nothing is lost; things stay T on average, some leaving early and some late.

\[L(t_0) = \text{input}(t_0)\,T, \qquad \frac{dL}{dt} = \text{input} - \frac{L}{T}, \qquad \text{DELAY1} = \frac{L}{T}\]

With a constant T this is the curve of SMOOTH. They differ when T changes: a delay keeps its contents L, so its outflow changes at once; a smooth keeps its output.

PySD.jl: delay(input, T), delay(input, T; init = initial_value).

DELAY3, DELAY3I, DELAY N

DELAY3(input, T) · DELAY3I(input, T, initial_value) · DELAY N(input, T, initial_value, n)

Material through n stages in a row, each of T/n: most of it comes out around T after it went in (construction, ageing chains). The larger n, the closer to a fixed delay.

\[\frac{dL_1}{dt} = \text{input} - L_1\frac{n}{T}, \qquad \frac{dL_i}{dt} = (L_{i-1} - L_i)\,\frac{n}{T}, \qquad \text{output} = L_n\,\frac{n}{T}\]

with $L_i(t_0) = \text{input}(t_0)\,T/n$ (or initial_value·T/n).

PySD.jl: delay(input, T; order = 3), delay(input, T; order = n, init = initial_value).

TREND

TREND(input, T, initial_trend)

The growth rate someone watching the input perceives, as a fraction per unit of time ("sales grow 5 % a year"): the input against its smoothed past.

\[A(t_0) = \frac{\text{input}(t_0)}{1 + \text{initial\_trend}\cdot T}, \qquad \frac{dA}{dt} = \frac{\text{input} - A}{T}, \qquad \text{TREND} = \frac{\text{input} - A}{T\,|A|} \;\; (0 \text{ if } A = 0)\]

For an input growing steadily at a rate g, TREND settles on g.

PySD.jl: trend(input, T; init = initial_trend).

FORECAST

FORECAST(input, T, horizon)

The input's perceived trend extended a horizon ahead, to plan capacity or investment.

\[\frac{dA}{dt} = \frac{\text{input} - A}{T}, \qquad \text{FORECAST} = \text{input}\left(1 + \text{horizon}\,\frac{\text{input} - A}{T\,A}\right)\]

PySD.jl: not translated yet. By hand: smooth(input, T) for A, and the formula.

INITIAL

INITIAL(value)

A value frozen at the start of the run: a reference level ("population in the base year"), or a starting value computed from other variables.

\[\text{INITIAL}(x)(t) = x(t_0) \quad \text{for every } t\]

PySD.jl: x(t₀).

Memory by counting steps

These move at the model's ticks, with any solver: the solver stops at every tick, where they record and move, and holds them in between.

DELAY FIXED

DELAY FIXED(input, T, initial_value)

An exact pipeline: everything comes out exactly T later, unchanged. A fixed construction or maturing time, a legal waiting period: a conveyor belt rather than a mixing tank.

\[n = \max\!\left(1, \operatorname{round}\frac{T}{Δt}\right), \qquad \text{output}(t_k) = \begin{cases} \text{initial\_value} & k < n \\ \text{input}(t_{k-n}) & k \ge n \end{cases}\]

n is fixed when the model is built: changing T needs a rebuild.

PySD.jl: input(t - T; init = initial_value).

SAMPLE IF TRUE

SAMPLE IF TRUE(condition, input, initial_value)

Follows the input while a condition holds and keeps the last value when it stops: a value fixed at a given year, the price at the last review.

\[\text{output}(t_k) = \begin{cases} \text{input}(t_k) & \text{condition}(t_k) \\ \text{output}(t_{k-1}) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}\]

and initial_value until the condition first holds. A condition such as t == 2020 is read at the ticks, so it holds there with any solver.

PySD.jl: sample_if_true(condition, input; init = initial_value).

RANDOM 0 1, RANDOM UNIFORM, RANDOM NORMAL, RANDOM EXPONENTIAL

Noise: demand fluctuations, measurement errors, Monte Carlo experiments. Vensim draws one number per step and keeps it for the step; the seed makes a run repeatable.

PySD.jl: not translated yet.

Switches at given times

Functions of the time. A model with them stops at every tick, and a solver's step that ends at a tick sees the time just before it, so a switch at a tick changes the rates from that tick on.

PULSE

PULSE(start, width): a one-off event of a given length (a two-year subsidy, a test shock).

\[\text{PULSE} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{start} \le t < \text{start} + \text{width} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}\]

PySD.jl: (start ≤ t < start + width).

PULSE TRAIN

PULSE TRAIN(start, width, interval, end): a repeating event (a yearly harvest, periodic maintenance).

\[\text{PULSE TRAIN} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{start} \le t < \text{end} \text{ and } (t - \text{start}) \bmod \text{interval} < \text{width} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}\]

PySD.jl: pysd_pulse_train(t, start, interval, width, end).

STEP

STEP(height, step_time): a permanent change from a given time (a new tax rate, a policy switch); also the standard test input.

\[\text{STEP} = \begin{cases} \text{height} & t \ge \text{step\_time} \\ 0 & \text{before} \end{cases}\]

PySD.jl: height * (t ≥ step_time).

RAMP

RAMP(slope, start, end): a gradual change between two times (a phase-in).

\[\text{RAMP} = \text{slope} \cdot \operatorname{clamp}(t - \text{start},\; 0,\; \text{end} - \text{start})\]

PySD.jl: slope * clamp(t - start, 0, finish - start).

Data series

GET XLS DATA, GET DIRECT DATA, and data variables: historical series or scenarios read from a spreadsheet or file. Between two data points, a straight line (or the value kept, looking forward, or the raw values); before the first and after the last, held.

PySD.jl: data(sheet, time, cells; method = :interpolate) as a parameter, called as series(t); data(parents, :name) for a series from another model's run or a file (Data from other models or from files).

Switches on values, limits and choices

IF THEN ELSE

IF THEN ELSE(condition, a, b): rules that switch (order when the stock is below target, start a policy once a threshold is passed). a if the condition holds, b otherwise.

PySD.jl: ifelse(condition, a, b); ifelse.(…) for arrays.

MIN, MAX

Physical limits: shipments can't exceed what is in stock, a flow can't go below zero (MAX(0, x)).

PySD.jl: min(a, b), max(a, b).

INTEGER, QUANTUM, MODULO

Whole units (power plants, people), rounding to a batch size, cycles such as the month within a year.

\[\text{INTEGER}(x) = \operatorname{trunc}(x), \qquad \text{QUANTUM}(x, q) = q\,\operatorname{trunc}(x / q), \qquad \text{MODULO}(x, m) = x - m\left\lfloor \frac{x}{m} \right\rfloor\]

PySD.jl: trunc(x), pysd_quantum(x, q), mod(x, m).

VECTOR SELECT, VECTOR RANK, VECTOR SORT ORDER, VECTOR REORDER

Working along a dimension: rank technologies by cost, pick the cheapest, add up only the selected elements.

PySD.jl: not translated yet (helpers for the older backends: pysd_vector_select and the pysd_vector_* functions).

ALLOCATE BY PRIORITY, ALLOCATE AVAILABLE

Sharing a limited supply among users who each ask for an amount, the highest priority served first.

PySD.jl: not translated yet (helpers for the older backends: pysd_allocate_by_priority, pysd_allocate_available).

Plain calculations

XIDZ, ZIDZ

Divisions that may hit zero (a share of a total that can be empty).

\[\text{XIDZ}(a, b, x) = \begin{cases} a / b & b \ne 0 \\ x & b = 0 \end{cases}, \qquad \text{ZIDZ}(a, b) = \text{XIDZ}(a, b, 0)\]

PySD.jl: xidz(a, b, x), zidz(a, b).

SUM, PROD, VMIN, VMAX, ELMCOUNT

Collapsing a dimension (the ! in Vensim): a world total from regions, the most expensive technology, the number of regions.

PySD.jl: sum_over(x, d), prod_over(x, d), minimum_over(x, d), maximum_over(x, d) (several dimensions: sum_over(x, (d1, d2))); length(d).

Lookups

A relationship known as a curve or from data (the effect of price on demand); its argument can be any variable. Between two points, a straight line; outside them, held at the first or last value.

PySD.jl: piecewise_linear(x₁ => y₁, x₂ => y₂, …) (WITH LOOKUP and lookups written in the model), lookup(sheet, x, cells) (GET XLS LOOKUPS, GET DIRECT LOOKUPS); called as f(x).
